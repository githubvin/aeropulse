# AeroPulse BRICS: Hackathon Pitch & 3-Minute Video Demo Script

**Event**: *Build with AI: Code for Communities (Second Edition)*  
**Organizers**: Google Cloud & Google Developer Groups (GDG) India  
**Track**: BRICS Sustainability, Resilience, Innovation & Cooperation  

---

## 1. The 30-Second Elevator Pitch

> *"Air pollution doesn't stop at national or municipal borders, but our monitoring systems do. When dense smog covers economic corridors like Delhi-NCR or South Africa's Highveld, authorities trade blame because satellites pass only once a day and border sensor data cannot legally be exported.  
> We built **AeroPulse BRICS**: an AI-powered, sovereign-preserving climate action platform. By pairing **Google Gemini 2.0 Flash** with reverse atmospheric back-trajectory physics, our **AeroTrace** engine pinpoints the exact culprit factories and fires upstream, measures the precise kilograms per hour crossing the border, and dispatches automated bilateral emergency alerts across BRICS nations."*

## 2. 3-Minute Video Demo Script (Step-by-Step)

### **[0:00 - 0:30] The Hook & Problem**
- **Visual**: Show the AeroPulse Command Center loaded on the Indo-Gangetic Corridor.
- **Narrator**: 
  *"Welcome to AeroPulse BRICS. Every winter, over 300 million citizens across trans-boundary corridors suffer from hazardous air quality. Today's monitoring tools suffer from a critical triad: macro-satellites have a 24-hour lag, ground sensors miss midnight industrial releases, and neighboring governments fight over who caused the pollution because neither has verifiable proof."*

### **[0:30 - 1:15] User's Core Innovation: AeroTrace Source Attribution**
- **Visual**: Click **"Inspect Source (AeroTrace)"**.
- **Action**: Select the *High-Temperature Industrial Coal Stack Plume* preset (or upload a photo). Click **"Execute AeroTrace Reverse Attribution"**.
- **Narrator**:
  *"Here is our core innovation: **AeroTrace**. When a citizen or border monitor flags heavy smoke, **Google Gemini 2.0 Flash** inspects the visual morphology—classifying it as industrial coal soot with 84% opacity.  
  Simultaneously, our physics engine inverts the Lagrangian advection equation against live meteorological wind vectors. In seconds, it calculates the plausible travel distance—**18.4 km upwind**—and identifies the primary culprit: Panipat Thermal Power Station, with a 92% match confidence and explainable AI reasoning."*
- **Visual**: Show the map drawing the cyan back-trajectory line, the amber dispersion cone, and the culprit marker.

### **[1:15 - 1:45] 48-Hour Predictive Plume Scrubber & Border Flux Gate**
- **Visual**: Click **"▶ Play"** on the bottom scrubber. Watch the plume advance to $+18\text{h}$ and $+24\text{h}$.
- **Narrator**:
  *"Instead of waiting for hospitals to fill up, our 2D advection-diffusion solver forecasts plume movement 48 hours in advance. Meanwhile, our **Virtual Border Flux Gate** calculates the exact transport rate—currently 1,420 kilograms of toxic mass per hour crossing the border—giving downwind cities a 24-hour head start to protect schools."*

### **[1:45 - 2:20] Sovereign Federated Learning & Policy Sandbox**
- **Visual**: Click **"BRICS FedMesh"**, then open **"Policy Sandbox"**.
- **Narrator**:
  *"To respect national data sovereignty, AeroPulse uses **Federated Learning**. All 5 BRICS nodes (India, South Africa, Brazil, China, Russia) collaboratively train our predictive models using FedAvg—sharing model weights, but zero raw domestic telemetry.  
  In the **Policy Sandbox**, leaders can drag curtailment sliders: cutting 40% of agricultural burning and 30% of industrial stacks saves 1,280 hospital admissions downstream."*

### **[2:20 - 3:00] OASIS CAP Emergency Alert & Call to Action**
- **Visual**: Click **"CAP Alerts"**, show the generated OASIS CAP v1.2 XML and multilingual broadcasts (Hindi, Portuguese, Chinese, Russian).
- **Narrator**:
  *"Finally, AeroPulse dispatches international-standard **OASIS CAP v1.2 alerts** simultaneously to both sides: alerting upwind regulators to inspect stacks and downwind health centers to deploy mist cannons.  
  Built with Google Cloud and Gemini AI, AeroPulse turns environmental finger-pointing into coordinated planetary climate action. Thank you."*

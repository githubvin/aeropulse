The Problem

The Blind Spot: Atmospheric Physics vs. Sovereign Borders

Air pollution doesn't respect political boundaries. Over 300 million citizens across BRICS economic corridors face severe seasonal health emergencies due to three critical systemic failures:

1. SATELLITE TEMPORAL LAG
Earth observation satellites (Sentinel-5P, MODIS) only pass 1–2 times daily and are completely blind under heavy cloud cover, rain, or thick winter inversion fog.
2. HYPER-LOCAL INVISIBILITY
Government monitoring stations are sparse and clustered in city centers, completely missing midnight industrial soot dumps, rural brick kilns, and border stubble fires.
3. THE BLAME GAME & SECRECY
Neighboring states and nations trade accusations because neither has verifiable proof of where plumes originated, and domestic laws prohibit exporting raw telemetry.
Problem Framing Indo-Gangetic • Highveld Basin • Pan-Amazonian • Bohai Rim

Innovation: AeroTrace

AeroTrace: Reverse Plume Triangulation & Source Attribution

Pairing Google Gemini 2.0 Flash Multimodal Vision with Lagrangian atmospheric back-trajectory physics:

1. GEMINI MULTIMODAL VISION
Analyzes citizen sky photos to classify particulate type (Industrial Coal Soot vs. Biomass vs. Chemical mist) and calculates optical opacity percentage and plume rise.
2. LAGRANGIAN BACK-TRAJECTORY
Numerically inverts wind vectors backward in time against live meteorology, computing exact plausible distance travelled (18.4 km) and transit time (1.25 hours).
3. UPWIND SECTOR FILTER
Constructs an upstream dispersion cone (±18°) querying registered industrial directories to rank candidate culprit factories with transparent, explainable AI reasoning.
AeroTrace Technology Google Gen AI SDK • Lagrangian Physics • Spatial Indexing
